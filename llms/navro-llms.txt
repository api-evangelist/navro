# Navro

> Navro (formerly Paytrix) is a London-based payments curation platform that lets large businesses move money globally through a single contract and API integration. Rather than stitching together a patchwork of banks and payment providers, Navro routes each payment via the right rail, currency and compliance model, executing millions of payouts across 140+ currencies and 200+ regions. It is authorised by the UK FCA, the Central Bank of Ireland and FinCEN in the US.

Note: As of this profile, Navro exposes no public developer portal, API reference, or OpenAPI definition. Integration is via the Navro API under contract and the no-code Navro Hub portal; developer documentation is gated. The links below are the company's public surface.
